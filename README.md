# News

## Overview

This is a collection of interesting and reliable news sources tailored for computer science students, computer scientists and those involved in the industry. It's also good for those who want to learn a lot more interesting terminology.

## Sources

- ACM: <https://technews.acm.org/>
- IEEE: <https://spectrum.ieee.org/topic/computing/>
- Quanta: <https://www.quantamagazine.org/tag/computer-science/>
- The Verge: <https://www.theverge.com/tech>
- TechCrunch: <https://techcrunch.com/tag/tech/>

## Motivation

This was made as I got tired of trying to find interesting news, then filtering out most news sites that are unreliable and manipulative[^1]. 

[^1]: There are sources that are "reliable" but will misrepresent information to manipulate the audience. Also take caution when using these sources, as these sites may have unknown minsinformation, change with time or change by acquisition.
